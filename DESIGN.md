# LABx design

Preserve the existing artwork-led interface. Customers buy memberships with bonus NFT raffle entries and partner discounts. Keep main pages concise; put explanations in `/guide`, transaction reviews and expandable details.

## Brand

- Use the supplied `web/public/brand/labx-logo.png` and favicon. Preserve source artwork bytes and display NFT images uncropped.
- Basetica is the body/control font. Uppercase Sixtyfour remains the main website heading style. The short promotional film uses larger Basetica text for rapid reading.
- Use full-opacity lime `#b9ff87`, purple `#b37df6` and pink `#ff79c0`. The cursor trail is mint `#87ffb1`.
- The page background is light with a 24px dot grid. Continuous grain has a pause control and a static reduced-motion fallback.
- Header logo sits above centered navigation. Menu items have no purple selection blocks. Wallet access uses an icon; chain and transaction context belong in the relevant workflow.

## Materials and layout

Ordinary buttons use a solid fill with a soft inset highlight/shadow, generous hit targets and visible focus. Keep their pillow emboss subtle and readable. Membership cards retain their approved smooth metallic gradients, embossed uppercase titles and black circles: purple Entry, bronze, silver, gold and lime Platinum. Avoid adding badges, repeated kickers or speculative dashboard statistics.

Use consistent page-top breathing room, clear groups and responsive wrapping. The footer stays simple and light with black links/icons. Its links must reach real pages. Mobile should retain the same hierarchy without clipped text or horizontal scrolling.

Explore supports empty, single and multiple authoritative listings. An unavailable connection is distinct from an empty catalog. Raffle details retain the artwork, accurate prices/fees/supply and a concise primary action, with recovery alternatives when eligible. Studio and Profile must expose the complete seller/buyer process, including claims and pagination. Never invent activity, balances, winners or partner redemption.

## Motion and accessibility

Text resolution and original scroll effects preserve semantic text and readable final states. Avoid copying paid reference source/assets. Use transforms and opacity, clean up observers/listeners and honor live reduced-motion changes. Content must remain usable without animation or JavaScript where applicable.

The 15-second splash explains membership purchase, bonus NFT raffle entries and partner discounts. It plays once on an initial homepage arrival, starts muted, has Sound and Skip controls, and bypasses direct routes, reduced motion and data-saving preferences. Failures release the page. Portrait and landscape compositions are separate exports.

Keep one h1 per page, native form labels and controls, keyboard navigation, clear errors/status, visible focus and at least 44px action targets. Artwork alt text describes the supplied work. Decorative motion must not obscure names, prices or transaction review information.
